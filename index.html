Unkn3wnDB — Stay Unknown.

Unkn3wnDB is a cybersecurity crew made up of hackers and security enthusiasts from different corners of the world, united by curiosity and a shared obsession with breaking things to understand how they work.

From Capture The Flag competitions and bug bounty hunting to vulnerability research and security experimentation, we're always learning, testing, and pushing our limits.

We just enjoy the process: digging deeper, questioning assumptions, chasing weird behavior, and occasionally finding something that shouldn't be there.

Different countries. Same curiosity. Stay Unknown.

// who we are

The collective

We started as a handful of friends who kept staying up late over the same puzzles. It was initiated by Sushant Mali, and it grew into a small, serious crew chasing the moment a system does something it was never meant to do.

We are not a company wearing a hacker costume. We are operators who happen to keep a tidy workbench. Our work is offensive security research: find the bug, write the proof, understand exactly why the machine broke.

Everything we touch is scoped and authorized. We go after the holes before someone with worse intentions does and we write it down so it gets patched, not traded.

// founder

Our founder

Sushant Malia.k.a. Jolicious

Sushant founded Unkn3wnDB with a simple vision: to bring together passionate hackers and build a team that competes in Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions around the world. What started as a CTF team grew into a community of like-minded security enthusiasts exploring ethical hacking, bug bounty hunting, and cybersecurity research.

As the founder, Sushant continues to shape the team's direction, encourage collaboration, and keep its focus on learning, improving, and taking on new challenges.
